# Wallpaper Credits

Garage ships 32 third-party wallpapers: 16 dark and 16 light. Copyright
remains with each respective creator. The wallpapers are licensed under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) and are not covered by
Garage's MIT License.

Full attribution, source links, original dimensions, acquisition dates, and
SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [`wallpapers.toml`](wallpapers.toml). That
manifest is the canonical source of truth for the wallpaper collection.

Attribution is not required by the Unsplash License, but Garage records and
publishes it to credit the creators properly and preserve asset provenance.
