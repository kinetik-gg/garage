# Bundled assets

The installed theme includes two assets copied from Garage's existing bundle:

- `background.jpg`: “an abstract black background with curved curves” by
  Andrew Kliatskyi, distributed under the Unsplash License. Source:
  https://unsplash.com/photos/ggykb5c7oeQ
- `PlusJakartaSans.ttf`: Plus Jakarta Sans, distributed under the SIL Open
  Font License 1.1. Its full license remains in Garage as `PJS-OFL.txt` and is
  installed beside the font.
